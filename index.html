簡介About

我的研究關注台灣基督教與社會變遷之間的關係，特別是宗教組織的發展、教會增長與衰退、宗教參與，以及教會的社會功能。

從早期的宗教認同與神學教育研究，到近年參與台灣教會普查、牧者調查及離堂者研究，我逐漸將研究興趣聚焦於宗教組織如何回應人口、文化與制度環境的變化。

目前正探索教會的組織生命歷程、永續性與社會影響力等議題，希望結合宗教社會學、組織社會學與實證研究方法，理解台灣基督教會的發展與轉型。

除了學術研究，我也關注研究成果如何協助宗教團體理解自身處境，並促進教會與社會之間的對話。

My research examines the relationship between Christianity and social change in Taiwan, with particular attention to the development of religious organizations, church growth and decline, religious participation, and the social functions of churches.

Beginning with early work on religious identity and theological education, and more recently through participation in Taiwan church censuses, pastoral surveys, and studies of church-leavers, I have increasingly focused on how religious organizations respond to changing demographic, cultural, and institutional environments.

I am currently exploring the organizational life course, sustainability, and social impact of churches, drawing on the sociology of religion, organizational sociology, and empirical research methods to understand the development and transformation of Christian churches in Taiwan.

Beyond scholarly research, I am also interested in how research findings can help religious communities understand their own circumstances and foster dialogue between churches and society.

研究歷程Research Journey

  • 2004–20082004–2008
    個人認同與宗教經驗以馮玉祥自傳文本研究為起點，關注個人認同如何受到歷史、政治與宗教環境影響。 Personal Identity and Religious ExperienceBeginning with a study of Feng Yuxiang’s autobiographical texts, I examined how personal identity is shaped by historical, political, and religious contexts.
  • 2010–20162010–2016
    神學教育與宗教組織從神學教育、本色化靈修與基督教公益活動，逐漸關注宗教組織如何回應文化、制度及社會環境。 Theological Education and Religious OrganizationsThrough work on theological education, contextualized spirituality, and Christian philanthropy, my attention gradually turned to how religious organizations respond to cultural, institutional, and social environments.
  • 2021–20252021–2025
    台灣教會的實證研究參與教會普查、疫情調查、牧者研究及教會增長議題，從實證資料觀察台灣基督教的組織變化。 Empirical Studies of Churches in TaiwanThrough church censuses, pandemic surveys, pastoral studies, and research on church growth, I trace organizational change in Taiwanese Christianity from empirical data.
  • 2026至今2026–present
    宗教參與、教會衰退與永續性透過離堂者研究及教會衰退研究，進一步探索宗教組織的生命歷程、適應能力與社會功能。 Religious Participation, Church Decline, and SustainabilityThrough studies of church-leavers and church decline, I further explore the life course, adaptive capacity, and social functions of religious organizations.
