簡介About

我的研究核心關注於台灣教會在快速社會變遷中的發展與挑戰，特別是如何在宗教市場與公共議題中持續見證信仰。方法上，試著結合統計分析、歷史分析、宗教社會學與神學詮釋，以跨學科的視角梳理教會與社會互動的模式。應用領域涵蓋教會發展、公共神學與神學教育，期盼能為本地教會提供具體的洞見與實踐資源。

My research centers on the development and challenges facing churches in Taiwan amid rapid social change — particularly how Christian communities sustain their public witness within a competitive religious marketplace and contested public sphere. Methodologically, I combine statistical analysis, historical analysis, the sociology of religion, and theological interpretation to trace patterns of church–society interaction from an interdisciplinary vantage point. My applied work spans church development, public theology, and theological education, with the aim of offering concrete insight and practical resources to local congregations.
